# Statements for Designing Fast, Efficient, or Correct Systems.

"A rational way of observing for thinking rationally."


## Assumptions
1: The statements require a competent statements evaluator/system designer/goal definer that is at least capable of logical inference and memory.

2: A System is a group of processes and processees in specific domains, connected by links.

3: Statements may recurse up or down to refine, redefine, or restructure the system design or its design goal.

4: The statements are not axioms, rules, or prescriptions, but observations of reality with widely overarching implications.


## The Statements, in no specific order

Epistemology: Unknowns risk becoming implieds, implieds risk becoming forgottens, forgottens risk becoming harms, harms threaten correctness.

Change: Dynamics risk unknowns.

Irreversibility: Every action is lossy in some way.

Tradeoffs: Efficiency, speed or correctness can be traded for the other two.

Boundaries: Process and processee are 2 halves of one whole linked together, how the whole is divided into Process and Processee can be influenced.

Links: Processes and processees can become faster and more efficient through optimized links between them.


## Use
The statements are not 6 tools, they are one tool meant to be applied at every part of the goal definition and system design.
The statements are meant to provide the right observation angles for defining the right goal and designing the right system for it.
